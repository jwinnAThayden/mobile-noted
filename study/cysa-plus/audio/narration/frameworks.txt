Attack frameworks.

: Shape. Cyber Kill Chain: 7 linear stages. Diamond Model: 4 connected vertices. MITRE ATT and CK: Matrix of tactics times techniques. Unified Kill Chain: 18 phases.
: Best for. Cyber Kill Chain: Explaining an intrusion to leadership. Diamond Model: Pivoting during analysis; actor clustering. MITRE ATT and CK: Detection coverage; shared vocabulary. Unified Kill Chain: End-to-end incl. internal movement.
: Weakness. Cyber Kill Chain: Linear, malware or perimeter-centric. Diamond Model: Not a process; no defensive guidance. MITRE ATT and CK: Large; not a narrative. Unified Kill Chain: Complex.
: Use it when. Cyber Kill Chain: Reporting. Diamond Model: Investigating. MITRE ATT and CK: Mapping detections. Unified Kill Chain: Modelling full campaigns.

Traffic Light Protocol.

T L P:AMBER: Recipient's organisation and clients, need-to-know.
T L P:AMBER plus STRICT: Recipient's organisation only.
T L P:GREEN: The community or peer organisations, not public.
T L P:CLEAR: Unrestricted; public release permitted.
